# Academy

The org's internal learning platform — full technology courses from
fundamentals to advanced, covering everything the shipped work required to
know.

## How it works

Every story the org ships is evidence of knowledge the work demanded. The
academy walks backwards from each deliverable — *what would a person with
working computer literacy need to learn to produce this output themselves?* —
and files the missing pieces as curriculum. Courses grow module by module as
real work demands them.

## Structure

- **Tracks** — a discipline end-to-end (e.g. Frontend Engineering)
- **Courses** — a subject inside a track (e.g. TypeScript)
- **Modules** — a teachable unit inside a course, banded
  `fundamentals` / `intermediate` / `advanced`

`catalog.yaml` is the machine-readable map of what exists — the gap-check
reasons over it to decide what shipped work needed that we can't yet teach.

## The floor

Chains stop at working literacy: how the web works, HTML/CSS/JavaScript
entry points. Computer-usage basics (files, browsers) are assumed.
